# HearHere Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 9, 2026
**Developer:** Podkos Studio
**Contact:** podkos.studio@gmail.com

## Summary

HearHere does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data. The app has no INTERNET permission, so it cannot send information off your device. It contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party libraries or SDKs.

## What the app does

HearHere reads the notifications on your Android phone aloud through a Bluetooth audio device you choose (or the phone speaker, if you choose it), using the text-to-speech engine built into your phone.

## Information the app can access

- **Notifications.** After you grant Notification access in Android settings, HearHere can see the app name, title and text of notifications as they arrive. This text is used only to be spoken aloud on your device. It is held in memory only for as long as needed to speak it. It is never saved to storage and never sent anywhere.
- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth).** HearHere uses this Android permission only to list your paired Bluetooth audio devices and to detect which are connected.
- **Battery optimization.** HearHere may ask you to exempt it from battery optimization in system settings. This involves no personal data.

## Information stored on your device

HearHere stores the following on your device only, in the app's private storage:

- your settings and preferences;
- the hardware addresses of the Bluetooth devices you tick in the app;
- the package names of apps that have sent you notifications, with the time of the most recent one, so the Apps screen can list them, and your on/off choice for each.

This information is never accessible to the developer. It is deleted when you uninstall HearHere or clear its storage in Android settings.

## Information collected or shared

None. HearHere does not collect user data, does not share data with anyone, does not sell data, and has no accounts.

## Text-to-speech engine

To speak a notification, HearHere hands the sentence to the text-to-speech engine you selected in Android settings (for example, Speech Services by Google). That engine is separate software with its own privacy practices, which HearHere does not control. If you want speech to stay fully on your device, choose a voice that works offline in your system text-to-speech settings.

## Children

HearHere is not directed to children and collects no information from anyone.

## Your choices

You can turn off Notification access at any time in Android settings (Settings > Notifications > Device & app notifications, or by tapping the status line in the app). You can remove all stored data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

## Security

Because HearHere does not transmit or collect data, there is no developer-side data to protect. Locally stored settings are protected by Android's app sandbox.
